    Rules & tips

    • Moving: click a piece then a highlighted square, or drag and drop. Dots show legal moves.
    • Castling: move your king two squares toward a rook. Not allowed if the king or that rook has moved, if pieces are between them, or if the king is in, passes through, or lands in check.
    • En passant: if an enemy pawn just advanced two squares and sits beside your pawn, you may capture it as if it had moved one square — but only on the very next move.
    • Check & checkmate: you must always get out of check. No escape means checkmate — game over.
    • Illegal moves are rejected and the board tells you why.
    • Draws: stalemate, insufficient material, threefold repetition and the fifty-move rule are all detected.
    • Learn: the Learn, Puzzles, Endgames, Openings and Review tabs teach and train everything — from how the pieces move to converting won endgames.
    • Keyboard (Play tab): U undo · F flip · H hint · N new game.
